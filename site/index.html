Built for a 16-pixel browser tab

Your CRM, booking pages and client sites will show the logo as a tab icon. Browsers draw it at 16 × 16 pixels, where lettering blurs and fine detail merges. Every mark here was drawn on a 24-unit grid and checked at that size.

How I critique a website

Looks are judged first. In Stanford's web credibility study, 2,684 people judged 100 live sites, and 46.1% based their judgement partly on visual design, more than any other factor. So the critique scores what people notice, what slows them down and what tells them you are real.
